# Yulu-Hypothesis-Testing

## Problem Statement

**Business background:** Yulu is India's leading micro-mobility service provider, offering shared electric cycles as a sustainable, first/last-mile commute solution across Indian cities. Yulu has recently experienced a **dip in revenues** and wants to understand what is driving (or limiting) demand for its shared electric cycles so it can plan operations, pricing and marketing more effectively.

**Business objective:** Yulu has hired a consulting company to identify the significant factors affecting the demand for these shared electric cycles in the Indian market. Specifically, the company wants to know:
1. Which variables are significant in predicting the demand for shared electric cycles?
2. How well do those variables describe the electric cycle demand?
